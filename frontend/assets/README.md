# Homepage assets

The authentic club crest is the user-supplied file `876F202D-5EA0-4B8E-BA5E-916D1021BA7D.png`, copied unchanged to `frontend/assets/vfl-logo.png`.

The following illustrative photographs were generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as 1200px WebP for delivery. They are not documentary photographs of VfL Ramsdorf's facilities. No external photo host is used. Vite fingerprints and bundles them into `assets/`.

- `frontend/assets/banner-games.webp`: photorealistic local German amateur football pitch at golden hour, white goal right, green grass, modest floodlights and trees, no people/text/logos; calm left and bottom for text overlay.
- `frontend/assets/banner-pitch.webp`: photorealistic local training pitch in warm afternoon light, football and orange cones on the right, grass, softly blurred goal and trees, no people/text/logos; calm left and bottom for overlay.
- `frontend/assets/banner-cabins.webp`: photorealistic modest club changing room, wooden bench on right, navy shirts on hooks, pale walls, tile floor, natural window light, no people/text/logos; calm left and bottom for overlay.

The navy gradients and all text are rendered separately in CSS/React. Source prompts used the photorealistic-natural classification and landscape 1536×1024 composition.
